# Getting started

These tutorials take you from an AI assistant answering from your documents to complete conversational journeys:

1. [Build a RAG bot on the Tock documentation](rag-tutorial.md): run Tock on your machine and answer questions from
   documents with an LLM, in about 30 minutes.
2. [Deploy a platform with Docker](run-platform.md), to run your own Tock platform.
3. [Create your first bot with Tock Studio](first-bot-studio.md), on the [demo platform](https://demo.tock.ai/):
   stories and answers written without code, to handle the journeys that must not be left to the LLM.
4. [Program journeys in Kotlin](first-bot-kotlin.md), to go beyond what can be configured in _Tock Studio_.

To understand how generated answers, stories and FAQs work together, see [How the bot answers](../gen-ai/how-it-works.md).
To connect your bot to a messaging channel, see the [Channels](../channels/index.md) section.
The main notions used in Tock (applications, intents, stories, RAG...) are explained in [Concepts](../concepts.md).

# The _Sentences Rag exclusions_ menu

RAG exclusions let Tock exclude topics from the scope of the AI answers provided by RAG.

![RAG Exclusions - Training](../img/gen-ai/gen-ai-rag-excluded-1.png "Configuration screen of the topics excluded from AI")

In addition to the prompt, they restrict the answers of the bot so that it stays within its scope and does not come up with overly inventive answers.

To add topics or sentences that the bot must not handle:

1. Go to the _Language Understanding_ > _Inbox sentences_ menu
2. Select the sentence you want to exclude
3. Click on "Exclude from RAG handling"

The _Gen AI_ > _Sentences Rag exclusions_ screen lists all the sentences / topics excluded from the bot answers.

![RAG Exclusions](../img/gen-ai/gen-ai-rag-excluded-2.png "Screen of the topics excluded from AI")
